# Design System

## Direction

Indian cultural, ceremonial, and contemporary. Use restrained mandala, rangoli, paisley, and gold-line details. Avoid sci-fi, neon-purple, or generic technology motifs.

## Palette

- Base maroon: `#2A0000`
- Deep maroon: `#3B0508`
- Gold: `#E8A33D`
- Bright gold: `#FFD700`
- Warm ivory: `#F8F1E4`

## Typography

- Main English title and classical wordmarks: Cinzel Decorative
- Hindi display title: Yatra One
- Body and interface: Inter

## Surface rule

The main page background is continuous through all sections. Do not place a full-width solid or translucent background behind a section. Use a deeper maroon surface only inside intentional framed panels/cards, with gold borders and subtle inner linework.

The continuous canvas uses the Hero's atmospheric maroon gradient, gold particles, and a subtle desktop mouse parallax effect.

## Motion

Use slow, subtle gold particle drift, mandala rotation, and short hover transitions. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
